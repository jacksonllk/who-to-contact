# Changelog

## v0.1.0 (20 Nov 2026)

First public release.

- **16 preset personas.** Each has a job title list built from how people-data providers group roles.
- **Any role you name.** Ask for exact titles or a team without a preset. When no one matches, the skill searches the team that usually leads that area.
- **Company check.** The skill confirms the company from its website, and retries by name when the website finds no one.
- **Work emails (add-on).** With Enrichable connected, each email is checked against the person's current employer.
- **X accounts.** Reply "X" after a search.
- **Lists of companies.** Upload a CSV and get back a CSV of the people at each company.
- **Results.** A table in the chat and a CSV in a `who-to-contact/` folder that git ignores.
